### 🌟 1. What to Add (High-Impact New Features)

#### A. Printable 1-Page Audit Memo / Dossier ("የግምገማ ማጠቃለያ ሪፖርት")
* **Concept**: In institutional public procurement, auditors and review committees need tangible documentation for physical files.
* **Feature**: A **"Print / Export PDF Audit Memo"** button inside the Record Detail modal that generates a clean, official-looking 1-page summary formatted with:
  - Record reference & buyer/supplier details
  - The visual **Price Benchmark Gauge**
  - The reviewer's recorded status, timestamp, and notes
  - The neutral verification checklist questions for human review.

#### B. Visual Procurement Timeline (የሂደት የጊዜ ሰሌዳ)
* **Concept**: Currently, approval and payment dates are shown as text dates (`18 Jul 2026`, `42 days`).
* **Feature**: A lightweight horizontal milestone stepper inside the modal:
  $$\text{Tender Advertised} \longrightarrow \text{Bids Received (4)} \longrightarrow \text{Award Approved} \longrightarrow \text{Payment Pending (42d delay)}$$
* **Why it helps**: Makes bottlenecks and overdue delays immediately visible at a single glance.

#### C. Interactive Threshold Settings Popover ("የማጣሪያ ገደቦች ማስተካከያ")
* **Concept**: Allow presenters and auditors to adjust rule-based sensitivity thresholds in real-time:
  - *Comparative Price Threshold*: e.g. toggle $> 1,500,000\text{ ETB}$ or $40\%$ vs $50\%$ variance
  - *Delay Threshold*: e.g. $30\text{ days}$ vs $45\text{ days}$
  - *Supplier Repeat Wins*: e.g. $2$ wins vs $3$ wins
* **Why it helps**: Greatly enhances live demos by showing how transparent rules dynamically adapt to agency policy changes.

#### D. Pre-Configured Sector Scenarios ("የዘርፍ ማሳያ ሁኔታዎች")
* **Feature**: A quick selector to load realistic demonstration presets:
  1. 🏥 **Health Sector ICT**: (Laptops & medical tech comparison)
  2. 🏗️ **Roads & Construction**: (Repeated supplier patterns)
  3. ⚡ **Rural Water & Energy**: (Solar lanterns & water pumps with overdue payment delays)

### ✂️ 2. What to Remove or Streamline

| Item | Recommendation | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monolithic Scrolling in the Detail Modal** | **Add In-Modal Tabs** (`Overview`, `Market Range`, `Human Review`, `AI Guidance`) | Currently, opening a record requires scrolling down past 4 large sections. Tabbed sub-views make inspection faster and cleaner on laptops and tablets. |
| **Redundant "Synthetic Data" notices** | **Consolidate into a sleek banner** | Currently, notice boxes appear in the top banner, table footer, modal header, and inside every explanation card. Consolidating into a consistent header badge keeps the interface clean while maintaining 100% compliance. |
| **Manual URL Entry Requirement in Market Range** | **Make URL optional, require observation date** | Reviewers often obtain phone quotes, local physical catalog prices, or framework agreement unit prices without a live web URL. Making the URL optional accommodates offline local market references. |

### ✅ Option 1 Complete: Printable 1-Page Audit Memo / PDF Dossier (`የግምገማ ሪፖርት`)

We have built and integrated the **Printable Public Procurement Audit Memorandum Dossier**:

1. **New "Audit Memo / Print (የግምገማ ሪፖርት / አትም)" Action**:
   - Inside any record detail view, a prominent action button allows reviewers to generate an institutional audit memorandum.
2. **Comprehensive Institutional Dossier Format**:
   - **Header**: Official emblem, bilingual title (*PUBLIC PROCUREMENT AUDIT MEMO / የመንግስት ግዥ ግምገማ ማጠቃለያ ሪፖርት*), Dossier Reference ID (`SYN-ET-2401`), and live Review Status badge.
   - **1. Contract Particulars**: Item description (EN & AM), Category, Buyer Entity, Supplier Entity, Total Contract Value (`ETB`), Bids Received, Approval Date, and Payment Status.
   - **2. Transparent Review Signals**: Color-coded signal breakdowns with the exact mathematical or date thresholds crossed.
   - **3. Market Reference Benchmark**: Summary of verified comparables (Low, Median, High unit prices, Record Unit price, and exact $\%$ variance).
   - **4. Authorized Reviewer Assessment**: The recorded status, review date, and contextual reviewer notes.
   - **5. Verification Checklist**: Actionable audit questions for tender committees.
   - **6. Official Sign-Off Block**: Designated lines for *Auditor / Reviewer Signature*, *Verification Date*, and *Official Agency Stamp Box*.
3. **Clean `@media print` Optimization**:
   - Formatted specifically for standard **A4 paper / PDF export** via browser print (`Ctrl+P` / `Cmd+P` or clicking **Print / Save PDF**). Navigation, UI controls, and background overlays are automatically hidden during print.
